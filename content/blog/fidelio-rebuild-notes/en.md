Fidelio began with a simple idea: stop screenshotting loyalty cards. The useful parts were never flashy - scan, organize, and open quickly.

## If I started again tomorrow

1. An **offline-first** list of cards.
2. A calmer home screen with fewer decisions.
3. Better empty and import flows before new features.

Project notes do not need a full changelog. Just the next honest step.
