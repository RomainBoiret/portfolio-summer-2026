This blog is for the work in between milestones: a pattern that finally clicked, a project that moved forward, or a tool worth sharing with someone else.

## Why write it down

School projects disappear into folders. Personal projects stall. Writing a few paragraphs forces a clearer story - what changed, what I learned, and what I would try next.

Not every post needs to be a tutorial. Sometimes it is enough to say:

> Here is what I shipped this week, and here is the part I am still working through.

## What belongs here

- **Learning notes** - concepts I am actively using, not every article I skim.
- **Project updates** - progress on work linked from this portfolio.
- **Useful finds** - libraries, patterns, or ideas I would recommend to a classmate.

If you are reading this, thank you for stopping by. More soon.
